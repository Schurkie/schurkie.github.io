Floatify – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

In short: Floatify stores your reminders and settings locally on your device. We do not run a server of our own, we do not sell your data, and we do not collect personal information beyond what is strictly necessary to deliver the features you choose to use. Google services (Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Play, AdMob, Firebase) are only involved when you use the feature that needs them.

1. Who We Are

Floatify is developed by Mark Bruining. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at: schurkiedev@gmail.com

2. What Data Floatify Uses

2a. Reminder data (stored locally on your device)

Reminders ("floats") you create — including title, message, date, time, color, and settings — are stored in the local storage of your device (Android SharedPreferences). This data leaves your device only if you enable Google Calendar sync or Google Drive sync (see sections 2c and 2d).

2b. App settings (stored locally on your device)

Your preferences such as bubble speed, quiet hours, vibration settings, shapes, per-app notification settings and the mode you chose (Simple or Advanced) are stored locally. They are not sent to a server of ours. If you enable Google Drive sync, your settings are included in the synced data (see 2d).

2c. Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect your Google account, Floatify uses:

  • Google Sign-In — to authenticate you.
  • Google Calendar API — to import your calendar events as floats (read access). If you turn on the option to push floats to your calendar, Floatify also creates and updates calendar events for those floats in your Google Calendar (write access to events).

Imported events are stored locally on your device. Floatify does not share your calendar data with anyone. You can revoke access at any time (see section 6). Google's own privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2d. Google Drive sync (optional, Advanced)

If you enable Drive sync, Floatify stores a copy of your floats and settings in the hidden App Data folder of your own Google Drive, so they can be shared between your devices. Only Floatify, signed in with your account, can read this folder. It does not appear in your normal Drive files and we, as the developer, have no access to it. Floatify requests only the Drive "app data" permission, not access to your other Drive files.

2e. Notifications from other apps (optional, Advanced)

If you grant Notification access, Floatify can turn notifications of the apps you select into floats, for example chat messages. Floatify reads the notification content (such as sender and message text) on your device to show it in the float and to let you reply from the float. Replies are sent through the original app. This content is processed on your device only and is never sent to us or to any third party by Floatify. You can switch Notification access off at any time in Android settings.

2f. Installed apps and app usage (optional)

Floatify can list the apps installed on your device so you can choose which apps' notifications become floats and give each app its own shape. If you grant Usage access, Floatify uses it to detect which app is currently in the foreground, so floats can behave correctly. This information stays on your device.

2g. Sharing text, widgets and adding to your calendar app

Text you share to Floatify from another app is turned into a float on your device. Home screen widgets show your floats and to-dos from local data. The "add to calendar" action hands the float's title, description and time to your calendar app through Android's standard screen; you decide there whether to save it.

2h. Firebase Cloud Messaging (optional)

Floatify uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications related to calendar sync. FCM may transmit a device token to Google's servers for the purpose of delivering notifications. No personal content from your reminders is transmitted via FCM. Google's privacy policy applies: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

2i. Wear OS sync (optional)

If you pair Floatify with a Wear OS watch, reminder data is transmitted to your watch using the Wearable Data Layer API (Google Play Services). This transfer happens between your phone and watch. No data is sent to our servers.

2j. Advertising (Simple mode)

The Simple (free) version of Floatify shows an ad banner provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and information about ad interactions. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Floatify asks for your consent before ads are loaded, using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later via "Ad privacy options" in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying Advanced removes the ad banner. Google's policies apply: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

2k. In-app purchase (Advanced)

Advanced is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details are processed by Google; Floatify never sees or stores your card or payment information. Floatify asks Google Play which purchases belong to your Google account in order to unlock Advanced (also after reinstalling), and stores a local flag on your device that Advanced is unlocked. The purchase is tied to your Google account.

3. Permissions Explained

  • Draw over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — to display floating reminder bubbles on your screen.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to show reminder alerts.
  • Notification access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE) — optional; to turn notifications of apps you select into floats (see 2e).
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE) — to keep floats active while the app is in the background.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — to trigger reminders at precisely the time you set.
  • Receive boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — to restore your reminders automatically after a device restart.
  • Query all packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) — to list your installed apps so you can choose which ones create floats and set a shape per app (see 2f).
  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) — optional; to detect which app is in the foreground (see 2f).
  • Calendar (WRITE_CALENDAR) — to add floats to your device calendar when you use the "add to calendar" action.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — used by Google AdMob to show ads in Simple mode (see 2j).
  • Internet (INTERNET) — used for Google Calendar and Drive sync, Firebase notifications, Google Play purchases and ads.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE) — to vibrate your device when a reminder appears (if enabled).
  • Ignore battery optimizations (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS) — to ensure reminders are not suppressed by the system when the screen is off.
  • Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK) — to briefly wake the device when a reminder fires.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to third parties. The only third-party services involved are provided by Google LLC, and only for the features described above:

  • Google Sign-In, Google Calendar API and Google Drive (when you connect your account).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging and Wear OS data transfer (when you use these optional features).
  • Google Play Billing (when you buy or restore Advanced).
  • Google AdMob (ads in Simple mode, according to your consent where required).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Reminder and settings data is stored on your device. You can delete it at any time by going to Settings → Apps → Floatify → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app. Clearing data does not remove your Advanced purchase; it is tied to your Google account and can be restored with "Restore purchase". If you used Drive sync, the copy in your Drive App Data folder stays until you delete it in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Floatify → Delete hidden app data).

6. Revoking Access

If you have connected a Google account, you can revoke Floatify's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Notification access and Usage access can be turned off in Android settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Floatify is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
