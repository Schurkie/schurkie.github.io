Floatify

Messages that float until you're done with them.

Floatify is an Android app that turns the notifications you choose into small floating bubbles over your other apps. A bubble stays on your screen until you have dealt with it: tap it to read the message, reply from the bubble, snooze it for later, or dock it at the edge of the screen.

What Floatify does

  • Floats from app notifications. Choose which apps (for example chat apps) may create a float. Floatify reads those notifications only to make the float.
  • Reply, snooze, dock. Answer a chat message right from the float, snooze it for a few minutes, or park it at the edge of the screen.
  • Your own floats. Write a reminder and keep it floating on screen until you finish it.
  • Google Calendar (optional). Sign in with your Google account to turn upcoming events from the calendars you choose into floats. This is optional; Floatify works without it.
  • Backup (optional). Save your floats and settings to a folder, or to the private app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • Simple or Advanced. Simple is one switch per app. Advanced adds your own floats, Google Calendar, shapes and themes.
Your privacy. Floatify keeps your floats on your phone and has no servers of its own that store your data. Read the full privacy policy and the terms of use.
